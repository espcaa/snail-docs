Welcome to Snail's documentation!

## What's Snail?

Snail is a plugin and theme loader for Slack that lets you customize and extend Slack's functionality. Parts of the architecture were inspired by [Taut](https://github.com/jeremy46231/taut/tree/main/core) - check it out too!
